How it works

Hydraulic head = measuring-point elevation − depth to water. All measuring points share the same fictional vertical datum. Readings represent static conditions in comparable screens.

The Python analysis checks dates, duplicates and finite values. Negative depth values can represent water above the measuring point. A missing value remains missing.

The annual range and first-to-last change are descriptive statistics. One year of synthetic measurements cannot establish a long-term trend or identify pumping or recharge effects.
