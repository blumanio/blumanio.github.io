Read the subsurface

A conceptual geological plan and a three-well head surface. Explore the network and the assumptions behind inferred groundwater flow.

Synthetic demonstration. All sites, measurements and geological units are fictional.

Local grid in metres · x east, y north · No geographic location

🟩 Sand and gravel🟨 Clay-rich coverDashed lines: head contours (m)

How it works

Fit a plane h = ax + by + c through three non-collinear, simultaneous head measurements. The gradient magnitude is √(a² + b²); the arrow points down the gradient.

The flow arrow assumes a connected, homogeneous, isotropic aquifer. All three wells are assumed to screen the same sand-and-gravel aquifer beneath any surface cover. Clay-rich cover is a fictional surface unit, not a calibrated aquifer boundary.

The contours extend the fitted plane beyond the monitoring triangle. Three points provide no independent validation; geological heterogeneity or anisotropy could change actual flow direction. No velocity or contaminant plume is predicted.
